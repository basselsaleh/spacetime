About this view

Gravitational-wave strain on a slice through the source: the orbital plane seen from above, a side view through the orbital axis, or any tilt in between (the sketch in the view panel shows where the slice sits). Color is r·h (the 1/r fall-off removed), divided by its peak (2,2) value in the orbital plane and scaled by a fixed factor, on a smoothly saturating scale.

For aligned spins the wave in the orbital plane is purely + polarized (h× = 0). h₊ > 0 (LAL convention) means space is stretched around the circle centered on the source and squeezed perpendicular to the plane. Off the plane both polarizations appear.

Spins tilted away from the orbital axis make the orbit precess: the orbital plane and its axis L̂ wobble around the total angular momentum J, which points up (+z), and the wave pattern swings with them. The spins are set at the start of the model's data (t = −4300 M), relative to the orbit there: tilt from L̂, azimuth from the direction to the heavier hole. Arrows show them as they precess. The views are fixed to J: the "top view" is the plane perpendicular to J.

Inside the ring is the near zone, where this far-zone formula does not apply. Black disks: horizons where the slice cuts them, at true size (at least a few pixels); faint outlines: the parts of holes hidden behind a slice. The orbital plane and phase come from the waveform model's own dynamics; the separation is post-Newtonian and schematic in the last orbits. Click a slice to move the probe (the ring marker).

The 3D view shows the strain throughout a sphere around the source, ray-marched like a glowing fog: crests (red, h > 0) and troughs (blue, h < 0) of the wave show as sheets, which for h₊ form the two spiral arms of the binary and move outward at the speed of light. Same normalization as the slices (r·h, so distant sheets are as bright as near ones); the near zone is left empty. h₊ and h× are measured against directions that wind around the vertical axis, so the sheets meet along it; a narrow cone around the axis is faded out. To keep it fast, the volume sums the three strongest mode pairs (the slices use all of them) and is drawn at reduced resolution, adapted to the frame rate.
