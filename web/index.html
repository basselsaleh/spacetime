About these views

Gravitational-wave strain seen from above the orbital plane (main view) and in a vertical slice through the orbital axis (side view). Colour is r·h (the 1/r fall-off removed), divided by its peak (2,2) value in the plane and multiplied by the gain, on a smoothly saturating scale.

In the orbital plane the wave is purely + polarised (h× = 0). h₊ > 0 (LAL convention) means space is stretched around the circle centred on the source and squeezed perpendicular to the plane. Off the plane both polarisations appear; the side view can show either.

Inside the ring is the near zone, where this far-zone formula does not apply. Black discs: horizons at true size (at least a few pixels), on post-Newtonian orbits derived from the wave phase; schematic in the last orbits. Click either view to move the probe.
